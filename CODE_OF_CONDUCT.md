# Community conduct

Communicate respectfully, criticize ideas with evidence, and welcome different experience levels.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, credential exposure and repeated disruption are unacceptable. Do not post private client material or sensitive personal information.

Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation. Use GitHub's Report abuse tools for conduct violations. Sensitive security vulnerabilities belong in the repository's private Security reporting channel; do not post confidential allegations in public issues.
